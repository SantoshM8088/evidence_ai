Northbridge Institute of Technology (fictional)
Merit Scholarship 2026-27 - Application Guide

Section 1. Submitting your application
Complete the online application form on the student portal and upload the required documents.

Section 2. Required documents
Applicants must submit the following documents with the application:
(a) an official academic transcript, and
(b) a copy of their valid student ID card.
Applications without both documents will not be reviewed.

Section 3. Queries
All scholarship queries are handled by the Merit Scholarship Review Committee. Please do not contact individual faculty members about scholarship decisions.

Section 4. Deadlines
The application deadline is published in the separate Merit Scholarship Deadline Notice.
